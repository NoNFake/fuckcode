Edit files with a file-oriented patch.

Envelope:
*** Begin Patch
[file sections]
*** End Patch

Sections:
*** Add File: <path>    every line is a + line (initial contents)
*** Delete File: <path>  nothing follows
*** Update File: <path>  in-place patch, optional *** Move to: <path>

Update hunks start with @@ and use -old / +new lines.

Example:
```
*** Begin Patch
*** Add File: hello.txt
+Hello world
*** Update File: src/app.py
*** Move to: src/main.py
@@ def greet():
-print("Hi")
+print("Hello, world!")
*** Delete File: obsolete.txt
*** End Patch
```

Always include the action header, and prefix added lines with +.
