Maintain a structured task list for multi-step work.

Use when: the task needs 3+ distinct steps, planning helps, the user gives multiple tasks, or new instructions arrive. Mark one item in_progress before working; mark completed only when the work and its verification are actually done.

Skip when: a single straightforward task, purely informational or conversational work, or tracking adds no value.

States: pending, in_progress (exactly one at a time), completed, cancelled.

Rules:
- Update status in real time; do not batch completions.
- Keep one in_progress while work remains; keep blocked work in_progress with a follow-up item.
- Preserve user-provided commands verbatim.
- Keep items specific and actionable; split large work.
